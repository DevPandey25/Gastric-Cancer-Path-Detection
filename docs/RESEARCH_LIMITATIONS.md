---

## 1. Key Scientific Limitations

1. **Patch-Level Prediction vs. Anatomical Diagnosis:**
   - Predictions generated by the classifier represent patch-level categorical estimates for an isolated $224 \times 224$ pixel tile.
   - The probability assigned to the `TUM` class reflects local tile morphology, **not** patient-level diagnosis or tumor stage.
   - Patch probabilities must **never** be interpreted as clinical cancer staging, tumor volume percentage, or clinical prognosis.

2. **Model Confidence vs. Clinical Certainty:**
   - Deep learning confidence scores (softmax probabilities) are mathematical model outputs subject to calibration error and out-of-distribution shifts.
   - High confidence on an artifact or blurred patch does not constitute clinical certainty.

3. **Absence of Patient-Level Partitioning:**
   - In public benchmark datasets where individual patient accession identifiers are withheld (such as GCHTID), tile-level train/test splits may contain patient-level overlap. Real-world generalization requires independent multi-center external clinical cohorts.

4. **Zero Synthetic Annotations:**
   - GastricPath AI enforces strict scientific integrity: because GCHTID distributes discrete tile labels without pixel-level contour masks, the 9-class segmentation infrastructure does not claim supervised training.
   - All cellular morphology outputs are designated as *candidate objects*, not verified histological nuclei.
